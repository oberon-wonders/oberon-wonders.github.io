Welcome to **Oberon Wonders' workshop**!

Here you'll find a glimpse of my work as a creator of worlds, narrative experiments and storytelling projects for the imagination of both adults and children.


## About Me

I am **Oberon**, a game master and creator of fantasy universes and role-playing games.

My workshop, **Oberon Wonders**, is dedicated to building worlds, role-playing games and miscellaneous adventures.  
I also build tools for game masters and players alike.


## Projects

### Worlds & Campaigns

- **[Fractured Worlds](https://oberon-wonders.github.io/oberon/fractured-worlds.html)**  
  A fairy-tale multiverse of four seasonal realms (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter) tied to the classical elements *(water, fire, earth, wind)* and shifting between paradoxical wonder, fairy dream, gothic horror, and winter folklore.

- **[Roncelin](https://oberon-wonders.github.io/oberon/roncelin.html)**  
  A magical forest where anthropomorphic animals embark on adventures in a playful fantasy setting designed for both children and adults.

- **[Farpoint](https://oberon-wonders.github.io/oberon/farpoint.html)**  
  A science-fiction RPG set in a vast network of orbital stations around Earth, where *Orbital Wardens* maintain the fragile balance of complex space habitats.

- **[Blackmoor](https://oberon-wonders.github.io/oberon/blackmoor.html)**  
  A gothic investigative adventure inspired by Ravenloft and classic ghost stories.
