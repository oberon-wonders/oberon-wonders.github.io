Fractured Worlds

A unique roleplaying game set in a fairy-tale multiverse of four seasonal realms tied to elements: spring (water), summer (fire), autumn (earth), winter (air) and ether. A philosophical, eery setting in a cycle of seasons shifting between paradoxical wonder, fairy dream, gothic child horror and winter folklore.

Roncelin

An easy to learn, playful roleplaying game set in magical forest where anthropomorphic animals – squirrels, hedgehogs, skunks, owls, bears and more – embark on funny and weird adventures designed for both children and adults. Family time full laughs and fantasy awaits.

Blackmoor

A dark fantasy and gothic investigative adventure inspired by Ravenloft and classic ghost stories. The ambiance is heavy, dark, horrific and is not for the faint of heart. Playable in several game systems, including Dungeons & Dragons 5e, or as a purely narrative shared storytelling.

Farpoint

An anticipation sci-fi exclusive roleplaying game, set in a vast network of orbital stations around Earth. A New Frontier where Wardens – prescient roaming judges and high-tech sheriffs – maintain the fragile balance of complex space habitats and population against the Emergence.

The Shroud of Dragons

A D&D 5e campaign, set in the Forgotten Realms, with a full draconic theme. Heroes will face many dangers, including intrigues of secret cults, to prevent the rise of Tiamat, the primordial dragon entity and her evil chromatic dragons.

Ars Arcana

A D&D 5e campaign et in the Forgotten Realms, at the heart of magic itself. Heroes will seek relics of an ancient civilization of fallen wizards, the powerful Netherese scrolls, to understand the facets of the Weave and its very nature, under the watchful eye of Mystra.

Axis Obscura

A Star Wars FFG campaign on the edge of the Empire, on the theme of spy infiltration. The heroes – smugglers, mercenaries and such renegades – will have no choice but to get in touch with the Rebellion in order to uncover the secret of an Imperial project that involves them.

Who am I, you ask?

Ah, silly me... I always forget to introduce myself to new apprentices.
I am Oberon, your friendly French neighbour ; interactive storyteller, worldbuilder, and game designer since 1481.
As a young squire, I was driven by a passion for creating maps of fantasy worlds, and roleplaying games. Ever since, I've strived to fuse that passion into marvelous creations, each with its own life. Doing so, I opened many a door to strange and unknown places, filled with wonders beyond imagination. And I am now back from my long-time travels to share them with you.

Stirring in a cauldron of classical litterature, fantasy, science-fiction, mythology and anthropology – delving into my curiosity about what makes us human – the inspiration I draw builds collaborative and immersive experiences for your pleasure, and mine indeed. From time to time, I also run masterclasses for game masters and players, helping them bring their own stories to life.
